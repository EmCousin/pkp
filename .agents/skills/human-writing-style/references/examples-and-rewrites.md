# Examples and Rewrites

Bad/better/best pairs for the rules that get violated most. Read these when you need to see how a fix actually lands.

---

## Reframe ban (the big one)

### Across sentence boundaries

Bad:
> Most teams think they have a hiring problem. They have a standards problem.

Better:
> The team's standards are unclear.

---

Bad:
> The dashboard looks like a reporting tool. It is really a decision filter.

Better:
> The dashboard filters decisions.

---

Bad:
> People blame the algorithm. The input data is broken.

Better:
> The input data is broken.

### Single sentence

Bad:
> It is not about the prompt. It is about the context.

Step 1 (delete the rejected half):
> It is about the context.

Step 2 (rewrite as a direct claim):
> Context controls the output.

### Rhetorical question form

Bad:
> Is this a productivity problem? No. It is an attention problem.

Better:
> Attention is the constraint.

---

Bad:
> The real question: how much control do you have?

Better:
> The useful question is: how much control do you have?

### Heading form

Banned headings:
- Not a tool. A system.
- Less noise, more signal.
- Beyond productivity
- From chaos to clarity
- The real problem
- What actually matters
- The hidden issue

Direct headings:
- The system
- Signal quality
- Attention limits
- Decision rules
- Input problems

## Analogy ban

### Replace with the literal claim

Bad:
> Your onboarding is a leaky bucket.

Better:
> Users leave during onboarding.

Best (specific):
> 42% of users leave on step 2 because the form asks for billing details before showing the product.

---

Bad:
> The product is a bridge between teams.

Better:
> The product lets sales and support see the same customer notes.

---

Bad:
> The strategy is a compass.

Better:
> The strategy says which customers to ignore.

---

## Specificity

Weak:
> The company faced challenges.

Better:
> The company missed payroll twice in 6 months.

---

Weak:
> The tool improves workflow.

Better:
> The tool removes 4 approval emails from the invoice process.

---

Weak:
> Users were frustrated.

Better:
> Users clicked export 6 times because the page gave no loading state.

---

Hypothetical (bad):
> Imagine a hypothetical scenario where a founder needs to clarify their messaging...

Real example (better):
> Example: a founder rewrites the homepage after 3 customers ask what the product does.

---

## Bloated verbs (copulative avoidance)

| Bad | Better |
|-----|--------|
| The report serves as a guide. | The report is a guide. |
| The app boasts a dashboard. | The app has a dashboard. |
| The framework offers a structure. | The framework has a structure. |
| The team plays a role in onboarding. | The team runs onboarding. |
| The new feature aims to reduce churn. | The new feature reduces churn. |
| This stands as a turning point. | This is a turning point. |

---

## Elegant variation

Bad:
> Sarah joined the company in 2021. The seasoned operator then led the team.

Better:
> Sarah joined the company in 2021. She then led the team.

---

## Puffery and significance inflation

Bad:
> The new pricing model marks a pivotal moment in the company's evolution, setting the stage for broader implications across the industry.

Better:
> The new pricing model raised average contract size by 18% in Q1.

---

## Meta commentary

Bad:
> In this section, I will walk you through the three key things to consider when evaluating a CRM.

Better:
> Three things to check when evaluating a CRM:

(or just go straight into the list)

---

## Engagement bait removal

Bad:
> Most teams ignore this. Let that sink in. Read it again.

Better:
> Most teams ignore this.

(if it matters, the reader doesn't need to be told to notice)

---

## Hype removal

Bad:
> This game-changing, cutting-edge platform will revolutionize your workflow and unlock unparalleled productivity.

Better:
> The platform replaces 4 tools and saves the average team about 6 hours a week.

## Final-pass walkthrough

Original draft:
> In today's fast-paced business environment, it is crucial to leverage data-driven insights to optimize your sales pipeline. The dashboard serves as a comprehensive solution that not only streamlines reporting but also empowers teams to make better decisions. Furthermore, the intuitive interface ensures seamless adoption across the organization.

Pass 1 (cut throat-clearing, dead openings, banned vocab):
> Use data to improve your sales pipeline. The dashboard simplifies reporting and helps teams make decisions. The interface is easy to adopt.

Pass 2 (specificity):
> The dashboard replaces 3 weekly status reports and shows pipeline coverage by rep. CSMs adopted it within 2 weeks of rollout.

Pass 3 (final read):
> The dashboard replaces 3 weekly reports and shows pipeline coverage by rep. CSMs adopted it within 2 weeks.

That's the version to send.
